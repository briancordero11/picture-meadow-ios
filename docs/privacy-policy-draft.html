What the app asks for

Wish Explorer helps people explore a wish using pictures and communication words. The current app does not ask for a child's name, birthdate, address, photographs, contact details, health information, or an account. It does not include advertising, tracking, analytics, or a social sharing service.

Choices on the iPad

Choices are kept in memory unless a grown-up turns on “Remember choices on this iPad.” If enabled, the app saves only the selected interest, park, rollercoaster color, and animal ideas. This local copy remains until it is cleared or remembering is turned off. Speech preferences are also stored on the iPad. The communication-board sentence is not saved.

The app uses the iPad's speech service to speak selected words. It does not record audio, request microphone access, or upload communication-board sentences to our service. Adult-gate answers are temporary and are not recorded or sent to a server.

Optional online descriptions

The default build works offline. If an online description service is enabled for a later build, an adult must choose that action and confirm the request. It sends only the wish category, park, color, and animal choices. The service also receives connection information such as the IP address and ordinary request metadata. The child's identity and communication-board sentence are not included.

The prototype service does not keep wish request bodies or generated descriptions. Its abuse-prevention limiter holds a connection address and request count in memory for a short period: normally up to 61 seconds with the default settings, with possible delays if the server is busy. It clears those entries when the process closes. No external AI image provider is connected in the current version. Adding one would require a new explanation of its role, data use and retention, and appropriate permission before sending information.

Clearing choices and changing your mind

A grown-up can turn off remembering or select “Start a fresh wish” to remove the current saved wish choices. Clearing the sentence removes the words in the current communication area. Choosing not to use the online feature does not prevent the offline wish journey. Device backups, if any, are managed separately through the device's backup settings.

Children and caregivers

The app supports a child-led experience with caregiver assistance. Adult controls are separated from the child's picture journey. The adult gate is an interface safeguard; it does not verify legal guardianship or replace any consent required for a particular use of personal information.
